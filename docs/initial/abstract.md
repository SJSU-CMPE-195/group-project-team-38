## ABSTRACT

**Meditag**
By Binh Nguyen, Jonathan Nguyen, Gurshan Warya

Medication errors are a significant problem in modern healthcare. Hospitals and medical centers rely on wristbands, patient records, and manual identity verification, and these processes are vulnerable to mistakes. The increasing complexity of medications and patient histories makes the safety checks behind this more challenging. With advancements in artificial intelligence and mobile technology there is an opportunity to make safer and more reliable methods for patient identity verification and care management.

Patients face risks with incorrect medications or procedures despite existing safety measures. This leads to overlooked allergies, misidentification, or incomplete patient information. Manual workflows are vulnerable to fatigue, time constraints, and communication gaps between healthcare staff. Current tools rarely allow clinicians to engage with patient histories beyond charts and records. This can result in inefficiencies in clinical workflows and risks to patient safety.

Our project, Meditag, is an AI-based system designed for patient safety and to support healthcare providers. We combine barcode scanning for room and medication verification with an interface that lets nurses and clinicians interact with patient histories through natural conversation. Our system provides real-time alerts for conflicts such as allergies, drug interactions, or dosage issues, while also helping staff to confirm patient identities. We aim to reduce errors and establish a safer standard of care in healthcare environments.
